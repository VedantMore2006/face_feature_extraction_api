Unlike Unipolar MDD (consistently downward trend), bipolar depression exists within a fluctuating mood framework.
This is instability + polarity shifts.

Temporal (related to time): Inconsistent response latency.

Noticeable inconsistency in reaction time across sessions.
Unlike unipolar MDD — delay is not stable.

4. Seasonal Depression (SAD)
(Seasonal Affective Disorder – depressive subtype)
Core theme:
Recurrent depressive pattern linked to seasonal cycles (typically winter).
Unlike unipolar MDD (continuous or episodic without seasonal trigger), this is time-of-year dependent variability.
Seasonal Expressivity:
Description: Reduced facial expressivity during the depressive season, with partial normalization in non-depressive months.
Clinical Meaning: Mood and affect decline seasonally (typically with reduced emotional reactivity), followed by improvement in other months.
Behavioral Meaning:
Fewer smiles and reduced emotional amplitude during the depressive season.
Face appears dull or less reactive in winter months.
Noticeable improvement during non-depressive season.
Emotional tone fluctuates annually, not randomly.
Contrast: Unlike dysthymia, the low state is not constant year-round.
Video Observable:
Lower AU12 activation and reduced AU variance during depressive months.
Higher emotional amplitude in non-depressive season.
Clear seasonal contrast in facial expressivity metrics.
Short Meaning: Emotional expression decreases during the depressive season and partially recovers in other months.
Psychomotor / Movement:
Description: Reduced head movement during the depressive season and season-linked reduction in psychomotor activity.
Clinical Meaning: Seasonal depressive phase includes psychomotor slowing and reduced energy.
Behavioral Meaning:
Slower head nodding and reduced gesture frequency during depressive months.
Lower overall motor energy.
Increased physical sluggishness in season-specific periods.
Improved movement energy outside the depressive season.
Contrast: Unlike chronic depression, the slowing is season-bound.
Video Observable:
Lower head velocity and reduced motion energy during depressive season.
Fewer gestures in winter recordings.
Increased movement metrics during non-depressive months.
Short Meaning: Movement slows during the depressive season and improves outside it.
Big Pattern Snapshot: Seasonal Depression (SAD)
Season-dependent reduction in:
Expressivity
Movement
Energy
Partial recovery in non-depressive months.
This represents cyclical temporal modeling at a macro time scale (months, not sessions).
